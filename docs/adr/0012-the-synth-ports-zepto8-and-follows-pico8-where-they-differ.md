# The synth ports zepto8 and follows real PICO-8 where they differ

The synth is a Rust port of the PICO-8 synth in zepto8 at commit `b2929a4` (`src/synth.cpp`, `src/filter.cpp`, `src/pico8/sfx.cpp`). It takes one fix from fake-08 at commit `814991a` (`source/Audio.cpp`, lines 764–765): zepto8 scales the 4-Tick echo by the REVERB 1 level, so REVERB 2 is silent there, and fake-08 scales it by the REVERB 2 level. Their licence notices ship in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES`, generated from `about.hbs`.

Where the PICO-8 reference set measures real PICO-8 differently from the port, the synth follows PICO-8, because an Exported Cart must sound the same as the Project in the tracker.

## Consequences

The synth departs from the port in these places, each measured on PICO-8 0.2.7:

- **Pitch.** Phase is kept in 65,536ths of a cycle and moves by a whole number of units per sample, taken from a one-octave table halved or doubled per octave. Those steps lie a little below equal temperament, and B is 7 cents below. During a slide, pitch moves in a straight line, and between two whole pitches PICO-8 takes the whole phase unit nearest that line. `Note.pitch` is wider than 0–63 so that a transposed Step plays at its true pitch.
- **Phase.** A new voice starts at phase 0, as every channel does when PICO-8 starts a song. Playing a sound keeps the voice's phase, and a music Pattern restarts the phase only of a silent voice. Channel 0's phase at Cart boot is 26,056/65,536 and `sfx()` keeps it. The last sample of each Tick wraps the waves back to the cycle they are in. A sound's position snaps to the whole-sample grid so rounding never crosses a note a sample late.
- **Second wave.** It moves at a measured ratio of the main wave: phaser 127/128 (125/128 with DETUNE 1, 254/128 with DETUNE 2); triangle 193/256 with DETUNE 1, 3/2 with DETUNE 2; everything else 255/256 with DETUNE; noise stands still. DETUNE 2 reads the second wave twice over, except on the triangle and the phaser. DETUNE on the phaser moves only its own second wave. Noise without DETUNE sets the second wave to the main wave at each Tick's start, so a phaser after it starts from there.
- **Shapes.** BUZZ on the saw lowers it by 1/8, then 1/24, on alternate cycles, counted from the cycle each Tick starts in. NOIZ holds each random level for (64 − pitch) samples. On a Waveform Instrument DETUNE 1 is 199/200 and DETUNE 2 follows the port (400/199); its two voices mix at 0.7 / 0.3 so the peak is unchanged, where Built-in Instruments mix at 5/6 and 5/12.
- **DAMPEN.** A one-pole low-pass that keeps 1/2 of the last output at DAMPEN 1 and 3/4 at DAMPEN 2. The level latches on the last sample of each Tick. A Custom Instrument's DAMPEN comes in at the end of its first Tick, at the higher of its level and the sound's, with no crossfade. A silent note keeps the sound's level, and an ended sound keeps only the level of a Custom Instrument it ended on.
- **Crossfade.** PICO-8 is already one sample into the crossfade on the first sample that changes.
- **Silent notes.** A silent note, or a fade-in's silent first Tick, resets the wave to phase 0 while the phaser's second wave holds. Under REVERB the waves instead run on at the silent note's pitch, as its Custom Instrument would play them when it is marked custom: a Waveform Instrument as a plain wave (an octave down with Bass), an SFX Instrument through its own notes from its first, a missing instrument as a blank SFX. A sound's echo rings through the silent notes after its last audible note, up to its LEN or last note, then is cut.
- **SFX Instruments.** The played note's current pitch, mid-slide included, adds to the instrument's own pitches before they become phase steps, and the played note's vibrato scales the result. Drop inverts the retrigger rule: the same pitch retriggers at the start of its note and a new pitch carries on. A slide carries on to its new pitch. Instruments do not nest: an SFX Instrument note inside an SFX Instrument plays as its Built-in Instrument.
