Chain alignment: lockstep vs free-running

PROTOTYPE, throwaway. Decision aid for Q1 of "How Export compiles a Project into a Cart" (adamico/picotracker#10).

The question. A Song row holds one Chain per Track, and each Chain is a list of Phrases. A PICO-8 Pattern holds one SFX per channel and ends when its left-most non-looping channel ends. How should parallel Chains become Patterns?

Lockstep (a): the k-th Phrase of every Track plays together as one Pattern. The next position starts when the longest one ends, and shorter ones go silent. Each Phrase is one SFX.

Free-running (b): each Chain plays its Phrases back to back, like the M8. Export cuts time wherever any Track's Phrase begins or ends, and splits Phrases into SFX at those cuts. A cut that lands mid-Step can't be exported, and a cut mid-arpeggio-group shifts the arpeggio.

Lockstep + repeat-to-fill (c): PICO-8's own model. A Cart is a flat list of Patterns, with no Chains. Each Pattern is 4 SFX that play together, and a looping SFX repeats until the Pattern ends. Here one Chain position is one Pattern, and a Chain entry marked ⟳ compiles to a looping SFX. The position lasts as long as its longest non-⟳ Phrase.
